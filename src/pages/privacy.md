This policy covers my website [miszori.dev](https://miszori.dev) and my apps [Mensaplan Zürich](https://mensa.miszori.dev), [amőba](https://amoeba.miszori.dev) and [vault](https://vault.miszori.dev). They are personal, non-commercial projects. I follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short: there are no accounts for visitors, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and I don't sell or share your data. Your settings stay in your browser.

## Cloudflare (all sites)

Every request to these sites goes through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which delivers them, protects them from attacks and provides HTTPS. miszori.dev is served entirely by Cloudflare. To do this, Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical details of each request, such as the address you open, the time, your browser (User-Agent) and the page you came from. Cloudflare acts on my behalf under its data processing addendum. For some data, such as security and abuse logs, Cloudflare is itself responsible; how it handles that data and how long it keeps it is described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

This can mean a transfer to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks, which the EU and Switzerland recognise as providing adequate protection.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in delivering the sites securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Cloudflare Web Analytics (miszori.dev and Mensaplan Zürich)

These two sites use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see roughly how they are used. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and doesn't build profiles. I only see totals, never individual visitors. A content blocker can stop it. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing whether and how the sites are used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Hosting (Mensaplan Zürich, amőba and vault)

These apps run on a rented server in a data centre in Hungary (EU). The hosting provider stores the data described below on my behalf.

A shared proxy in front of the apps logs connections, but because all traffic comes through Cloudflare, it only sees Cloudflare's addresses, not yours.

## Mensaplan Zürich

- **Server log.** The web server logs each request: your IP address, the time, the page, your browser (User-Agent), the page you came from and the country Cloudflare reports. I use it to run the app and find errors or attacks. The log is limited to 30 MB, and the oldest entries are deleted automatically when it is full.
- **Settings and favourites** (campuses, diet, allergens, budget, language, starred canteens and dishes) are stored only in your browser (local storage) and never sent to the server.
- **Location.** If you sort canteens by distance, your browser asks for your location. It is used only in your browser to work out distances and is never sent or stored.
- **Offline menus.** The app saves the menus of every day it offers (up to ten), with their photos, in your browser so it works offline. They contain no personal data.

The legal basis for the log is my legitimate interest in a secure, working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## amőba

- **Username.** To play online you choose a username. It is shown to the other players in your games and rooms, and checked against a list of offensive words.
- **Game data.** Your session, rooms and moves are kept only in the server's memory and never written to disk. A session is deleted 10 minutes after you leave (1 minute if you never chose a name), and everything is gone when the server restarts.
- **IP address.** The server keeps your IP address in memory while you are connected, to limit how many connections and new sessions one address can open. It is not logged or stored.
- **Your browser** keeps your username and a session token (local storage), so you can reconnect to a game.
- Games against Amőbot and on the same device run entirely in your browser and send nothing to the server.

The legal basis is providing the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## vault

I use vault to share files and text with specific people.

- **Contents.** Files and text are encrypted in my browser before upload. The key is only in the link, so the server stores only encrypted data and can't read it. A package is deleted when it expires, after its one download if it is a one-time link, or when I delete it.
- **Retrieval record.** When you download or reveal a package, the server records the time, your IP address and the browser, operating system and device details your browser sends (User-Agent and client hints). The record is encrypted so that only I can read it. I use it to see whether and when a package was retrieved and to spot misuse of a leaked link. It is kept until 90 days after the package expires.
- **IP address.** The server also uses your IP address in memory to limit failed requests. The web server's log doesn't contain IP addresses or browser details.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in sharing files securely and knowing whether they arrived (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Cookies and browser storage

None of these sites set cookies. Mensaplan Zürich, amőba and miszori.dev (for the light or dark theme) store settings in your browser's local storage only so the site works as you set it up. You can delete them at any time in your browser's settings for the site.

## Who receives data

Only Cloudflare and my hosting provider, as described above, and only on my behalf. I don't sell data or pass it on, unless the law requires me to.

## Do you have to provide data?

No. But without your IP address and the technical request data, the sites can't be delivered to you, and without a username you can't play amőba online.

## Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, and have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can ask for your data in a portable format. These rights come from the GDPR (Art. 15–20) and the FADP. Send your request to [gergo@miszori.dev](mailto:gergo@miszori.dev). It is free.

To find log entries or retrieval records about you, I need details such as your IP address and the time of your visit, because I can't otherwise tell which entries are yours.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC)](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch), in the EU the authority of the country where you live or work.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

## Your right to object

**Where I process your data based on my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).** I then stop, unless I have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for legal claims. Send your objection to [gergo@miszori.dev](mailto:gergo@miszori.dev).
